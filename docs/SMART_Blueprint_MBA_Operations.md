# SMART — Role Assessment Blueprint: MBA — Operations
### Domain → Sub-Domain → Concept, weighted, mapped to L1–L5

**Grounding note:** Operations MBA hiring is described as stable in 2026 indices — not the fastest-growing track, but a consistently reliable one, with supply chain and logistics technology roles specifically growing within traditional manufacturing, and quality management (Six Sigma/TQM) remaining a stable, named demand area.

**Calibration note:** concept-level weights are the calibration panel's first-pass judgment, to be validated against real regional hiring data.

---

## Level weightage summary

| Level | Composition |
|---|---|
| L1 | 40% general aptitude (quant-weighted) + 20% Domain A + 20% Domain B + 20% Domain C |
| L2 | 25% Domain A (applied) + 30% Domain B (applied quant) + 25% Domain C (applied quality) + 20% Domain D |
| L3 | 100% Domain E |
| L4 | AI-assessed threshold model — artifact/case-work upload + adaptive simulation defense |
| L5 | 50% checklist + 30% practitioner judgment + 20% presentation |

## Level 5 — Capstone
A real operations problem — a bottlenecked process or a supply-chain disruption scenario — diagnosed and resolved end-to-end with a defensible recommendation, presented and defended. Scored 50% checklist / 30% practitioner judgment / 20% presentation.

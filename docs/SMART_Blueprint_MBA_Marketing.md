# SMART — Role Assessment Blueprint: MBA — Marketing

### Domain → Sub-Domain → Concept, weighted, mapped to L1–L5

**Grounding note:** marketing with a digital focus remains a consistently named top-demand MBA specialization in 2026 hiring indices, alongside sustained enterprise demand for candidates who can translate consumer data into positioning and campaign decisions rather than only execute channel tactics.

**Calibration note:** concept-level weights are the calibration panel's first-pass judgment, to be validated against real regional hiring data.

---

## Level weightage summary

| Level | Composition                                                                                                                   |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| L1    | 40% general aptitude (verbal/quant-weighted) + 20% Domain A + 20% Domain B + 20% Domain D                                     |
| L2    | 30% Domain A (applied sizing/segmentation) + 25% Domain B (applied campaign judgment) + 30% Domain C (metrics) + 15% Domain D |
| L3    | 100% Domain E                                                                                                                 |
| L4    | AI-assessed threshold model — artifact/case-work upload + adaptive simulation defense                                         |
| L5    | 50% checklist + 30% practitioner judgment + 20% presentation                                                                  |

## Level 5 — Capstone

A real, scoped go-to-market or repositioning brief, worked end-to-end — segmentation, positioning, a metrics-based success plan — presented and defended. Scored 50% checklist / 30% practitioner judgment / 20% presentation.
